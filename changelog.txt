**Blocky Outline v1.1.1** for **Minecraft 1.21.x** (Fabric). Lightweight and built with Fabric API.

**What's new in version 1.1.1:**

- **Compatibility**: Built for **Minecraft 1.21 - 1.21.11** on **Java 21+**.
- **Smooth Transition**: Ultra-responsive LERP interpolation with smart auto-snap logic.
- **Zero-GC Math**: Eliminated per-frame allocations with independent clamp & bitwise HSV engine.
- **Dynamic Decoupling**: Reflection-based menu invocation preventing classloader incompatibilities.
